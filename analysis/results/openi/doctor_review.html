Chest X-ray report review

We are testing whether AI models can rewrite chest X-ray reports into plain language a patient can understand. Each original report below is followed by several plain-language versions (A, B, C…) in random order; they don't reveal how they were made, so please judge each on its own.

Part 1 has 4 versions per report. Part 2 has more versions per report (one from each of several AI models), so it takes a little longer per report.

For each version, check: accuracy (anything wrong or misinterpreted?), invented information (anything not in the original?), missing information (anything clinically important left out?), understandability, and whether it could be given to a patient. Short notes are very helpful.

The reports are from a public, de-identified research dataset (Open-i); "XXXX" marks text removed during anonymisation. Your answers are saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the page and come back. When finished, press Download my answers and send us the file.
